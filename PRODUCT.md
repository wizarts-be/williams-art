# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript; animation libraries are allowed (GSAP/ScrollTrigger, smooth scroll). Frontend only for now: forms and the newsletter pop-up are visual, with no backend. Possible later conversion to a WordPress block theme (Wizarts `wp-builder-agent`), so content must stay data-driven (one work = one record).

## Users

Anyone with the means and interest to own or commission a large sculpture: private owners of (large) gardens, companies, municipalities, event organisers. No single segment is prioritised. They arrive curious about the work, often from Google (garden-sculpture searches), Instagram or having seen a piece at an exhibition, and want to see the work at scale and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio and contact point for William Roobrouck (brand: Williams), sculptor in Laarne, Belgium. Show the work so it speaks for itself, and make it effortless to ask about a piece or a commission. Success = people browsing the works and contacting William.

## Positioning

One maker does everything himself, from first sketch to last weld, in his own studio, at monumental scale (up to 9 m, tonnes of corten steel), with credentials neighbours cannot claim: Tomorrowland fire pillars (2012), RHS Hampton Court silver medal (2019, now at RHS Garden Wisley), "Proud" bought by Knokke-Heist, work for ArcelorMittal and Fujirebio.

## Operating Context

Works are sculptures and objects in corten steel, steel, bronze, light and fire. Each unique object is numbered and comes with a certificate of authenticity; some exist in editions per size. Commissions involve site visit, design, fabrication, foundation, transport and installation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prices are never shown: always "prijs op aanvraag".
- Languages: NL, FR, EN, DE eventually; design is done in NL first.
- Old URLs of the three SEO pages (cortenstaal-tuinkunst, abstracte-beelden-tuin, grote-tuinbeelden) and their content must be preserved.
- No newsletter exists yet; only a design of a newsletter pop-up for now.
- Undecided: category assignment of works (awaiting William), texts for ~15 works without description, events since 2023.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Williams", wordmark lowercase "williams"; slogan "The Love story of art & craft" (never translated); work titles never translated.
- The brand designer's sketch `info/williams-website.html` (based on wschupfer.com/london) was approved by the client: its style is binding (white/black, Inter + Barlow Condensed + Outfit wordmark, moving photo strips, list/image toggle, scattered grids). Improve it to feel more upscale and more informative, don't replace it.
- Voice: warm, personal, proud of craft; home/contact/about in first person (William), SEO pages in third person.

## Evidence on Hand

- 34 works with photos, making-of photos and partial specs: `data/content.json`, `assets/origineel/werken/`.
- Videos: portrait of William, Fujirebio project, work clips: `assets/origineel/video/`.
- Real references listed under Positioning; RHS and ArcelorMittal articles linked in content.json.
- No testimonials, no prices, no client logos, no vector logo, no high-res originals yet. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; interface recedes.
2. Every image leads to more work or to a conversation.
3. Information is precise and factual (material, size, weight, edition), never sales talk.
4. Calm gallery restraint over spectacle; motion serves the sculpture.
5. Easy to extend: every new work follows the same structure.
