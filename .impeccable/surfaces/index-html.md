Scope: whole site, NL first (home, werk-index, werkpagina, 3 categoriepagina's, opdrachten, over, agenda, contact, nieuwsbrief-popup). Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience: anyone with budget for sculpture; job: see the work at scale, then ask about a piece. Proof: Tomorrowland, RHS silver, Proud 9 m Knokke-Heist, Fujirebio, ArcelorMittal. Prices never shown ("prijs op aanvraag").
Constraint: the brand designer's sketch info/williams-website.html is binding (user: "kijk naar de index.html en de voorbeelden"); layout follows it, refined with wschupfer.com/london and ulyssesdesanti.com.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The approved Schupfer-style sketch, made gallery-grade: the sculpture leads, the interface whispers. Refuses the standard artist-site of hero slider + card grid + text blocks.
OWN-WORLD: White ground #fff, ink #000, mute #8a8a8a, hairlines #e6e6e6, soft #f7f7f7; one corten accent #9a4a24 used only for tiny signals (new-dot, active marker, focus). Type (user-chosen 25/11, no serif): Tenor Sans for wordmark, titles (uppercase, tracked ~.16em), nav (uppercase, ~.22em) and big numbers; Jost 300/400 for body, meta and specs. Lowercase "williams" wordmark in Tenor Sans. Replaces the sketch's Barlow Condensed/Inter/Outfit. No cards, no shadows, no radii; captions sit under images in small Inter; mixed aspect ratios at fixed heights; asymmetric placement with large air.
STORY: Visitor meets the work in motion, recognises scale and seriousness (monumental commissions), browses list or image index, opens a work with facts and making-of, and asks about it in one field.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Ulysses-style intro: wordmark assembles letter by letter, then lifts to the header. Big "williams" left of center at ~19% inset with slogan "The Love story of art & craft" in small mute text; below it the first photo strip enters, tiles at varied heights and vertical alignments. Header: burger + nav left, small wordmark + "• Nederlands" right. Primary action: every tile is clickable; "Contact" is always visible in nav.
FORM: Choice 2 on the ordered list (refined moving strips, the sketch); user pinned it, overriding the roll. Seed key 842d9454. Signature interaction: strips drift continuously and speed up/reverse with scroll velocity, pausing under the cursor; between strips, Schupfer-style scattered single works with slow parallax and a "proof" row of monumental commissions with measured numbers (9 m · 3,5 t).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
